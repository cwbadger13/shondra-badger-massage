Meet Your Therapist

Shondra Badger, LMBT

Shondra has been a licensed massage and bodywork therapist for 15 years. She specializes in deep tissue and gua sha, and her clients stay with her for years — because the relief lasts.

She trained at the Southeastern Institute in Charlotte, graduating in 2011, and holds a Bachelor of Science in Psychology from Penn State University — an understanding of the mind-body connection she brings into every session. She is licensed and fully insured in North Carolina.
